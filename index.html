The Problem

Why Emergency Command Centres Stumble

During floods, industrial blazes, or urban accidents, dispatchers face high cognitive overload from asynchronous noise.

Fragmented Feeds

Critical SOS calls arrive simultaneously on Twitter, WhatsApp, police 112 lines, and local weather radar. No single operator can correlate them fast enough.

Fake & Duplicate Spam

40% of emergency social posts during storms are 3-year-old viral images, rumors, or 50 callers reporting the exact same overturned truck.

Misallocated Vehicles

In high confusion, three boats get sent to one minor waterlogged alley while 20 people trapped on a collapsing roof receive zero assistance.
